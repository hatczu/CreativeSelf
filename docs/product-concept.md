PRODUCT CONCEPT

CreativeSelf is a creativity, wellness, personal development, and employee engagement platform. It helps individuals discover their creative personality, find activities and learning experiences suited to them, and connect with relevant service providers.

For businesses, CreativeSelf provides customized employee engagement solutions designed to improve creativity, productivity, employee well-being, company culture, talent attraction, and retention.

The platform is powered by Acumen, an AI system that analyzes creativity and personality assessment results to recommend suitable creative activities, service brands, and learning plans.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a visually engaging, creative, premium, and highly polished experience.

The design should feel:

Creative but not childish
Energetic and inspiring
Modern and premium
Human and expressive
Professional enough for corporate clients
Visually diverse and editorial
Focused on discovery and personal growth

Avoid making the interface feel like a generic corporate SaaS dashboard. CreativeSelf should feel like a combination of a premium lifestyle platform, creative discovery service, learning platform, and employee engagement solution.

Use strong visual storytelling, expressive imagery, interesting layouts, generous whitespace, bold typography, subtle motion, and visually distinct sections.
